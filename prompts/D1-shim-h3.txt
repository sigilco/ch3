You are implementing ONE API-compat shim for `@sigilco/ch3` — a minimal typed Node-HTTP router authored inside the "scriptc subset" (rules below are law). The shim lets users keep their handler signatures: a pure re-mapping layer, zero code from the real framework.

**If core is missing**: create `test/stub-<yourshim>.ts` implementing the frozen `Ch3Core`/`Context`/`createRouter` contract from DESIGN.md — minimal in-memory implementation good enough to run your shim's unit tests under `node --test`. **Verification**: `node --test` on your shim tests + a scriptc compile smoke of `import ... from '../shims/<x>'` → `scriptc build` WITHOUT --dynamic must produce a serving binary (your shim + the stub must both be subset-clean; stub is test-only, but keep it compilable anyway). Report any subset-rule deviation you were forced into.

**Scriptc subset (verified rules — violations fail CI)**: sync handlers only; `null` not `undefined` in unions/returns; plain `({})` records (no Map/Set/Object.create(null)/freeze/class-as-dict/namespace-values); indexed `for` only, `for…of` on arrays OK, `for…in` never; `String(x)`/`Number(x)` entry-coercion for `unknown` params; NO `new Function`/`eval`; no `instanceof` vs non-program classes; no `new.target`; `try/catch new URL` not `URL.parse`; no object/method/module-exports shorthand forms; `import * as http from 'node:http'`; concretely-typed tables (no `any[]`/`unknown[]` — a ServerResponse can't cross an any-typed call); `res.end` as statement; body via `req.on('data')` collect + join; plain ESM only; `.d.ts` = concrete types + `| null` unions + zero DOM symbols.

**Docs promise**: every shim states its coverage target, its divergences list, and throws `"async handlers unsupported under ch3"` when `h.constructor.name === 'AsyncFunction'` at wrap time.

**Deliverables**: commit + push your branch; attach your shim source files, tests, MERGE-NOTE (if stubbed), and a 10-line summary listing exact divergences you implemented vs DESIGN.md's promise.
